# Terminology

Status: `PROVISIONAL`

Terminology is provisional pending prior-art alignment. These are current
internal working terms, not claims that the terms are standard or that the
definitions are final.

## Harness

An engineering-control layer around a coding agent that shapes execution,
verification, state, authority, or feedback. The final boundary between a
Harness and a Runtime/Host remains an open research question.

## Runtime / Host

The environment that supplies agent-loop, session, tool, sandbox, process,
extension, and other execution primitives. A Host capability is not by itself
Governance authorization.

## Repository Harness

Project-scoped controls grounded in repository authority, contracts,
validation, and change boundaries. The term is provisional and may overlap
with existing repository tooling.

## Task Harness

Task-scoped controls that bind an objective to a bounded execution and
verification context. It is not assumed to be a standalone runtime.

## Thin Governance

Governance limited to stable semantic, normative, authority, and evidence
boundaries while leaving commodity execution machinery to the Runtime/Host.

## Dynamic Harness

A Harness whose controls can vary with live state, evidence, risk, uncertainty,
or task horizon. Whether this requires a distinct system is unresolved.

## Adaptive Engineering Control

The provisional research label for adapting engineering controls to the
current state and decision needs of an AI software-development task. It is not
a novelty claim or a confirmed external term.

## Execution Finding

A structured observation about execution, failure, uncertainty, or a control
gap that may inform a later decision. A Finding does not automatically create
authority.

## Control Consumption

Evidence that a defined control was actually resolved and used at the relevant
execution boundary, rather than merely being present in a document or prompt.

## Runtime Admission

A proposed boundary at which an intended action is checked against applicable
authority, controls, and evidence before a consequential effect. Its final
ownership and implementation status are unknown.

## Owner Actionability

Whether a result or Finding gives the responsible owner enough specific,
relevant information to take an appropriate next action.

## Decision Value

The expected usefulness of information or a control for changing a decision,
reducing material uncertainty, or avoiding a costly failure.

## Done Enough

A scoped completion judgment tied to the current owner, claim, lifecycle, and
risk. It does not mean maximal imaginable completeness.

## Right-sized Assurance

Assurance effort proportionate to context, risk, claim strength, lifecycle,
and available evidence. It is a research hypothesis, not a settled method.

## Maker / Checker

A separation between producing an artifact or action and independently
checking it. The required independence and the useful envelope are still
subject to research.
